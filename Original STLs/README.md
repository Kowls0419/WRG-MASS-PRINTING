# Original STLs

28 unique mesh exports from all 78 source files in KY, JL, TC, WIP, archive and _backups. No unreadable sources.

One copy per geometry; duplicate printer/material versions removed. Current parts and archived revisions are here. Experimental cut pieces and interface/helper shells are in Experimental/. Multi-copy experimental stacks were split into connected shells and deduplicated.

These are STL exports of the embedded source meshes, not guaranteed byte-for-byte original CAD files. Coordinates and orientation come from the representative source mesh; dimensions are in millimetres. Plate placement, print settings and generated slicer supports are omitted. Different triangulations remain separate. Deduplication uses triangle coordinates at 0.001 mm resolution with translation and proper axis rotations ignored. Full provenance is in sources.json.
