# 2026-10-19 — Lightweight topboard for 60 bots

Six lightweight topboard print projects for **KY, TC and JL**, in **white JP PETG** or **white official Bambu PETG Basic**. Made from Kyle’s supplied `topboard.3mf`, with bottom-board print settings carried over.

**Assets contain only the six topboard `.3mf` projects.**

- **150 × 160 × 2 mm**, one board per plate, flat on Textured PEI at **KY’s saved bed position: X 128 mm / Y 88 mm**, matching across all six files. Supplied geometry and holes preserved.
- **Bottom-board process:** 0.3 mm layers / 0.2 mm first layer, 3 top / 3 bottom shells, 7% infill, by layer, no supports or brim. Bottom-board speeds retained.
- **Skirt:** 1 loop, 2 mm from the board, first layer only.
- **Walls:** KY/TC 3 × 0.82 mm; JL 4 × 0.62 mm for matching thickness.
- **JP PETG:** team profiles, 230°C nozzle / 225°C first layer, bed 65°C, flow limit 28 mm³/s.
- **Official Bambu PETG Basic:** matching factory profile, 250°C nozzle / 245°C first layer, bed 70°C, High Flow limit 23 mm³/s.
- **TC:** filament 1 uses the left 0.8 HF nozzle.

**60 boards = 60 plates.** An even split is **20 plates per printer**, using about **1.92 kg with JP PETG** or **1.89 kg with Bambu PETG Basic**. Times are slicer estimates and exclude handling between plates.

Open the matching project as a fresh file in Bambu Studio, select white filament matching the file’s material, slice and print.
