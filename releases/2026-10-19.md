# 2026-10-19 — Lightweight topboard for 60 bots

Three lightweight topboard print projects for **KY, TC and JL**, all in **white official Bambu PETG Basic**. Made from Kyle’s supplied `topboard.3mf`, with bottom-board print settings carried over.

**Assets contain only the three topboard `.3mf` projects.**

| Owner | Printer / nozzle | Download | Per-board estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| KY | P2S 0.8 HF | [Download .3mf](https://github.com/Kowls0419/WRG-MASS-PRINTING/releases/download/2026-10-19/KY-Topboard-Lightweight_P2S_0.8HF_TexturedPEI_BambuPETG.3mf) | ~42 min / 31.1 g |
| TC | X2D 0.8 HF | [Download .3mf](https://github.com/Kowls0419/WRG-MASS-PRINTING/releases/download/2026-10-19/TC-Topboard-Lightweight_X2D_0.8HF_TexturedPEI_BambuPETG.3mf) | ~40 min / 31.1 g |
| JL | P2S 0.6 HF | [Download .3mf](https://github.com/Kowls0419/WRG-MASS-PRINTING/releases/download/2026-10-19/JL-Topboard-Lightweight_P2S_0.6HF_TexturedPEI_BambuPETG.3mf) | ~54 min / 32.5 g |

- **150 × 160 × 2 mm**, one board per plate, centered and flat on Textured PEI. Supplied geometry and holes preserved.
- **Bottom-board process:** 0.3 mm layers / 0.2 mm first layer, 3 top / 3 bottom shells, 7% infill, by layer, no supports or brim. Bottom-board speeds retained.
- **Walls:** KY/TC 3 × 0.82 mm; JL 4 × 0.62 mm for matching thickness.
- **Official Bambu PETG Basic:** 250°C nozzle / 245°C first layer, bed 70°C, High Flow limit 23 mm³/s. TC uses the left 0.8 HF nozzle.

**60 boards = 60 plates.** An even split is **20 plates per printer**, using about **1.89 kg** of filament total. Estimated machine time: KY 14 h 01 min, TC 13 h 26 min, JL 17 h 52 min, plus handling between plates.

Open the matching project as a fresh file in Bambu Studio, select the white PETG Basic filament, slice and print.
