# 2026-09-30 — Active parts only; JL motorstand on 0.4 SF

This release only has the parts in active printing: the two bottoms and JL's new motorstand.

**JL**
- `JL-motorstand_P2S_0.4SF_TexturedPEI_BambuPETG` — **new**. Motorstand for a P2S with a 0.4 standard-flow nozzle, in Bambu PETG Basic black on Textured PEI at 70 °C. It's converted from the 0.6 HF version with the same wall and shell thickness: 6 walls (~2.7 mm), 5/5 shells (1.0 mm), 0.2 layers, 20% gyroid. Filament settings are Bambu's official PETG Basic 0.4 profile (250 °C, max flow 15, retraction 0.4). It's 36 copies per plate, about 383 g and ~12 h 27 m. **Not yet test-printed at 0.4 mm.**
